Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know any Korean to use this app?

Not at all. AlphaCombi converts every Korean letter into alphabet letters you already know. If you can read English, you can read along with K-POP lyrics.

Is this a pronunciation guide?

Not exactly. AlphaCombi is based on the Revised Romanization of Korean (RR), the official standard used on Korean road signs and government documents. It shows you the letters, not every sound change (like linking or nasalization) — because showing those would obscure the letter-assembly structure. Think of it as a reading cheat sheet for chanting along, not a full Korean lesson.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once you've saved your lyrics in the app, you can convert and practice anywhere — on the subway, on a plane, or right outside the venue. No WiFi needed.

Is it free?

Yes, AlphaCombi is free to download and free to use for converting lyrics. No sign-up required.

Can I save my favorite songs for a concert?

Yes. Build your concert setlist inside the app and load any song with a single tap. Perfect for the night before the show.

Does the app include lyrics?

No. AlphaCombi is a converter, not a lyrics database. You paste lyrics from your favorite source (Genius, Melon, Naver, etc.), and the app converts them. Tap the globe icon inside the app to search lyrics on the web.
